## WHO I AM

**Graphic Designer. Visual Thinker. Linux Enthusiast.**

I am the creator behind Mockify Lab, a creative space where visual design meets experimentation. I enjoy building distinctive brand identities, crafting meaningful visual experiences, and exploring the possibilities of customizable Linux desktops.

My approach is simple: design with intention, build with curiosity, and keep refining until the details feel right.
